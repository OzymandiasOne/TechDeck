# =====================================================================
#  THE PUPPET MASTER AT THE TABLE
#  What he says while you play him. Edit freely.
#
#  HOW TO EDIT
#    key | emotion | what he says
#
#    key      when he says it (the list is below). Several lines with the
#             same key are a pool: he picks one, never the same twice running.
#    emotion  calm, curious, amused, pleased, displeased, grave, narrate.
#             The emotion is what his face and the screen DO while he talks:
#             pleased brightens him, displeased scrambles his face and dims
#             the room, grave turns the light behind him to ember.
#             NARRATE is his other voice: he tells it as a story, second
#             person, past tense, three short beats. Shown in the narrator's
#             type, not the master's. Use it for the road, not the table.
#    holes    {name} is the card's name, {n} a number, {what} 'power' or 'health'
#             (the fire), where it makes sense.
#    pages    a long line breaks into pages at " // ". Each page types out,
#             then the next follows at the same pace, always.
#    glow     a page may start with a tag like [scale]: while that page
#             shows, that thing on the table glows. Tags: lanes_you,
#             lanes_him, scale, bell, piles, remnants, costs (every card in
#             hand with a price), cost_icon (the raised card's price).
#    auto     a page tagged [auto] (or [bell,auto]) does not wait for a key:
#             it holds a moment after typing and lets go by itself.
#
#  THE FILE HOT-RELOADS. Save it, play on. No restart needed.
#
#  VOICE RULES: cold, declarative, never uncertain, never apologetic,
#  never explains itself twice. He STATES. No em-dashes. The narrator
#  voice is the same mind telling it later: "You did this. Then that."
#
#  KEYS - the table
#    welcome            the table appears (first fight of a run)
#    welcome_again      the table appears (a later fight)
#    rules_lanes        the first time you look down at the board (first fight of your first run)
#    rules_blood        the first time you reach for a card you cannot pay for (same)
#    first_turn         the tutorial: your first turn begins
#    second_turn        the tutorial: your second turn begins (the draw, again)
#    first_draw         the tutorial: you made your first real draw; he points at the votary
#    first_hit          the tutorial: the first time one of his wounds one of yours
#                       ({yours} {his} {n} are the cards and the damage)
#    first_sacrifice    the first time you offer a real creature (not a votary)
#    sacrifice          you offered one card
#    sacrifice_many     you offered two or more at once
#    play_new           you played a card he has not seen this fight
#    play_repeat        you played the same card again this fight
#    play_small         you played something cheap
#    play_big           you played something costing 3 or more
#    play_{card id}     you played that card (votary, scarab, hound, huginn,
#                       weigher, gorgon, ouroboros, cerberus, sleeper,
#                       monolith, dead_star, mote, hydra, thornback, watcher,
#                       martyr, locust, wraith, crowned, seraph, leviathan)
#    commit_{card id}   he slid that card into his incoming row
#                       ({n} = how many of your cards it could strike from there)
#    hit_him            your strike landed on the scale ({n} = the weight)
#    hit_you            his strike landed on the scale
#    kill_his           you killed one of his
#    kill_yours         he killed one of yours
#    pet_dies           you killed his hound. He minds.
#    venom              a venom kill, either side
#    thorns             thorns bit a striker
#    guard              your sentinel stepped across
#    spawn              a locust made another
#    undying            your Ouroboros came back
#    grow               a Dead Star woke
#    deck_empty         your deck is empty at the start of a turn; only votaries remain
#    famine             you had nothing left to draw; hunger took his row
#    famine_again       ...and again
#    first_win          you won your first fight ever
#    win                you tipped the scale
#    teeth              you won with weight to spare ({n} teeth)
#    lose               he tipped the scale
#    candle_out         a candle went out (you have one left)
#    boss_welcome       the last table of the road
#    boss_phase         you tipped the scale on him once; he starts again
#    run_won            you beat the road
#    dead               the last candle went out
#
#  KEYS - the forge (the deathcard). The FIRST death of all uses the
#  "_first_time" keys; every death after uses the plain ones. In a
#  "_first_time" key the LAST page is the recap: it shows after you have
#  chosen, with the holes filled from your choice.
#    deathcard_first_time        the forge opens (first death ever)
#    deathcard                   the forge opens (later deaths)
#    deathcard_cost_first_time   choose the cost ... recap: {cost} {card}
#    deathcard_cost              choose the cost
#    deathcard_power_first_time  choose the numbers ... recap: {power} {health} {card}
#    deathcard_power             choose the numbers
#    deathcard_sigil_first_time  choose the sigils ... recap: {sigil} {card}
#    deathcard_sigil_first_time_double   same, when that card carries two sigils: {sigil} {sigil2} {card}
#    deathcard_sigil             choose the sigils
#    deathcard_before_named_first_time   before you type its name (first death ever)
#    deathcard_before_named      before you type its name (later deaths)
#    deathcard_post_named        you named it ({name})
#    digitize                    the card is taken; you are kept
#
# =====================================================================
